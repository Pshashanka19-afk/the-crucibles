About Us & Our Hackathon Journey

Team The Crucibles • NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2025

Our 1st Ever Hackathon

The Crucibles at NASA Space Apps 2025

Weather Foreseen was conceived, architected, and built by team The Crucibles during the NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2025. This was our team's very first hackathon!

Faced with the challenge of transforming complex planetary satellite data into everyday utility, we tapped into 40 years of daily solar, temperature, and precipitation measurements from the NASA POWER API. By marrying SARIMAX time-series statistical forecasting with Google Gemini AI natural language synthesis, we built a platform where anyone can query upcoming or historical weather with simple conversation.

During the hackathon, we navigated real-world data science challenges—such as handling convergence over 14,000 daily observations, non-linear monsoon distributions, and web pipeline optimization—yielding a resilient, production-ready system.

NASA Satellite Telemetry

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using The Crucibles.

How accurate are the predictions?

Our predictions use advanced meteorological models and AI algorithms to provide highly accurate forecasts. Accuracy varies based on the time range, with short-term forecasts being more precise than long-term predictions.

Can I check weather for any city in the world?

Yes! Our Weather Planner supports predictions for cities worldwide. Simply enter the city name and select your desired date.

How far in advance can I predict weather?

You can access weather predictions up to one year in advance from the current date, allowing you to plan long-term events and trips with confidence.

What does the AI chatbot do?

Our AI chatbot provides personalized weather insights, answers questions about weather patterns, and offers recommendations based on your queries.

How does current weather tracking work?

The current weather feature uses your device's location services to detect your position and display real-time weather conditions for your exact location.
